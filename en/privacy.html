Privacy

This site collects nothing about you unless you write to me. There are no cookies, no analytics, and no fonts or scripts loaded from third parties.

Who is responsible

Daniel Butnar, Brașov, Romania. E-mail: daniel.butnar@gmail.com. I am the controller for any personal data processed through this website.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Like every web server, GitHub receives your IP address and the addresses of the pages you open, and may keep them in short-lived technical logs to run and protect the service. I have no access to those logs. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website. Details and the safeguards for transfers outside the EU are described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact form and e-mail

If you use the contact form, the data you enter (name, e-mail address, company, website address and your message) is transmitted by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and delivered to my mailbox. FormSubmit uses the data only to deliver the message. Writing to me directly by e-mail works the same way, without FormSubmit.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps you request before a contract) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering enquiries). I keep messages as long as needed to reply and, if we work together, for the duration of the project and the statutory retention periods. Other enquiries are deleted within twelve months.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Your rights

  • Access to the personal data I hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Erasure (Art. 17) and restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20)
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
  • A complaint to a supervisory authority, for example the Romanian authority ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro) or the authority in your own country

To exercise any of these rights, send an e-mail to the address above.
